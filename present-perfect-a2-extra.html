Present Perfect A2: Fill in the Gaps

Positive

1. Joey (eat) five sandwiches today.
2. Ross (be) to the museum many times.
3. Peppa (lose) her teddy bear.
4. George (find) a big dinosaur bone.
5. We (visit) Astana three times.
6. Suzy Sheep (just / arrive) at the party.
7. I (finish) my homework.
8. They (see) this movie before.
9. Rachel (buy) a new dress.
10. You (clean) the whole kitchen!

Negative

1. Joey (not / finish) his pizza yet.
2. Ross (not / see) Rachel today.
3. Peppa and George (not / be) to the zoo.
4. I (not / hear) that song before.
5. We (not / meet) Suzy’s parents.
6. She (not / call) me back yet.
7. They (not / buy) the tickets.
8. George (not / stop) crying.
9. You (not / eat) your breakfast.
10. It (not / rain) this week.

Interrogative

1. Joey (ever / be) to Italy?
2. Ross (find) his keys?
3. Peppa (finish) her dinner?
4. you (ever / see) a real dinosaur?
5. they yet?
6. Suzy (tell) you the secret?
7. we (watch) this episode already?
8. I (leave) my phone here?
9. Chandler (write) the email?
10. it (stop) snowing?
